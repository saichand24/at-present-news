Privacy Policy

This policy covers the AT PRESENT NEWS Android app.

App preferences and cached stories

The app stores your selected district, language, theme and some recently loaded stories on your device. The district setting filters stories and the app does not request access to your device location. You can clear these settings and stored stories through Android’s app storage settings or by uninstalling the app.

News feeds and original stories

The app connects directly to third-party publishers to retrieve feeds and load available story media. Those services can receive the network information needed to respond, such as your IP address. When you open a publisher link, the publisher’s own privacy practices apply.

Advertising and choices

The app uses Google AdMob to display ads and Google’s User Messaging Platform to manage ad privacy choices. Google’s advertising SDK can collect and share information such as IP address, advertising or device identifiers, app interactions and diagnostic data for purposes including advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Data collected by the SDK is encrypted in transit. Where available, use Settings → Ad privacy choices in the app. Android also provides advertising ID controls.

Read more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Support messages and privacy requests

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use it to respond to and handle your request. You can use the contact email above to ask about the handling or deletion of your support correspondence.
